I'm a fashion and textile designer based in New Delhi. I graduated with a
B.Des in Fashion Design from the National Institute of Fashion Technology
(NIFT), where I specialised in couture and styling and built a practice
around handloom, embroidery, and considered, craft-led cloth.

Since then I've designed for **MAKU Textiles** in Kolkata and for **péro**
in New Delhi, and I'm now Lead Fashion Designer at **Dressfolk**, a handloom
label built around India's textile heritage. Alongside that I keep my own
collections — from MAKU's letters-to-summer palette to the defence-brat
story of B.R.A.T — working in handloom, jamdani, block print, and aari and
banjara embroidery, made in small craft clusters across India.

I'm available for freelance and collaborative work — commissions,
collections, and textile projects.
