I'm a fashion and textile designer based in New Delhi. I graduated with a
B.Des in Fashion Design from the National Institute of Fashion Technology
(NIFT), where I specialised in couture and styling and built a practice
around handloom, embroidery, and considered, craft-led cloth.

Today I design at **péro**, the New Delhi label known for its slow,
hand-detailed womenswear. Alongside it I keep my own collections — from
MAKU's letters-to-summer palette to the defence-brat story of B.R.A.T —
working in handloom, jamdani, block print, and aari and banjara embroidery,
made in small craft clusters across India.

I'm available for freelance and collaborative work — commissions,
collections, and textile projects.
