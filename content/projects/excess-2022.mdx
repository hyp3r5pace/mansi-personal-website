## Excess 2022

Building a collection of easy-to-wear garments using handwoven fabrics
sourced from across West Bengal, India. The goal was to try permutations of
fabrics — keeping in mind their weights and the quantity available — and to
ensure repeats in the final collection, letting nothing go to excess.
