## Executive Athleisure — épøque | évolution

A brand concept for high-functioning athleisure that moves between active,
executive, and street wear. The AW23 story, *Neo*, takes its cue from Flint
Collective NYC's post-pandemic light installations — reclaiming the darkened
street as a place of discovery — translated into work-to-play convertible
garments with utility pockets and hi-vis detailing.
