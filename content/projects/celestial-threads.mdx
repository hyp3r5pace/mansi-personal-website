## Celestial Threads

An Indian-wear collection drawn from space, zodiacs, and astrology.
Constellations and moon phases become the motif language; the signs are
reworked into aari embroidery and block-printed grounds, then carried across
kurtas and co-ords.

The deck moves from storyboard and motif studies through embroidery sampling
and surface detail to the final styled looks.
