## Concept

A study in restraint. Where traditional jamdani fills the field with
floating motifs, this set leaves the cloth quiet — one motif per
column, then breath. The weavers initially resisted; they came around
once the first sari came off the loom.

## Process

Cartoons drawn at full scale, walked through the loom by hand. Three
months from first sketch to finished cloth.
