## Maku Textiles — Exploring Aari

Setting up the first aari embroidery unit at the design house — exploring the
techniques and experimenting with colour palettes to establish an embroidery
language in line with the brand's design sensibilities.

These initial explorations helped me understand the technique involved, the
skill of the karigar, and the scope of aari in creating motifs for the Maku
AW23 collection.
