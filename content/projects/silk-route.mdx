## Concept

The collection began with a single question — what would the trade goods of the
silk route look like if they were redrawn for an Indian woman today? Six pieces,
each one a translation of a motif from a different stop along the route.

## Process

Sketches were translated to wooden blocks in Bagru. The dye work happened over
nine weeks during the monsoon, which is the only season the master dyers will
touch indigo here. Every meter of cloth was washed in the river.
