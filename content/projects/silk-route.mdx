## Concept

The collection began with a single question — what would the trade goods of
the silk route look like if they were redrawn for a woman dressing today?
Six pieces, each one a translation of a motif from a different stop along
the route.

<PullQuote attribution="A master block-printer, Bagru">
  The indigo decides when it is finished. We only listen.
</PullQuote>

## Final pieces

Six garments total — three made-to-measure tunics, two saris, one
lightweight summer jacket. Each carries a small embroidered tag with the
date the cloth was finished and the village the dye work happened in.
