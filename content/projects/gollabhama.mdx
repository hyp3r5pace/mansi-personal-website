## Gollabhama

A craft-based product development project: taking *Gollabhama* — the Telangana
handloom tradition of dancing-figure motifs — and translating it into a denim
textile built for streetwear. The brief was to choose a regional craft and
push it somewhere new while keeping it recognisably itself.

The work moves from the motif library — borders, booties, the pot-carrying
figures — through colour and weave studies to a final extra-weft denim fabric,
expanding the Gollabhama product library beyond the sari into everyday cloth.
