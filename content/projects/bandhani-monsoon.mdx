## Concept

The collection was sized for the body in monsoon weather: loose at the
shoulder, ankle-length, sleeves you can push to the elbow without
fighting the cloth. Every piece is bandhani, tied by hand in Kutch.

## Process

Tying took six weeks across two villages. Dyeing happened in three
batches between the first and second monsoon spells, so the indigo
could cure between rains.
