## Banjaran

Banjara embroidery from Telangana feels like the earth learning to sing —
thick stitches marching with intent, mirrors catching fragments of sky,
coins whispering of journeys and camps left behind. It is nomadic memory
sewn into cloth: bold, defiant geometry softened by the patience of the
hand, where crimson and indigo argue and then make peace.

<PullQuote>
  Nomadic memory sewn into cloth — defiant geometry softened by the patience of the hand.
</PullQuote>

Print and colour are drawn from the cotton, silk, and sico fabrics shared
by the client, reworked into a contemporary desi range.
