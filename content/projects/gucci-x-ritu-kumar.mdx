## Gucci × Ritu Kumar

A conceptual AW-RTW collection responding to fashion's long history of
cultural appropriation — sparked by Gucci's much-criticised kurta, sold for
lakhs without crediting its Indian roots. Rather than appropriation, the
project proposes *collaboration*: pairing Gucci with the Indian heritage
house Ritu Kumar, so the craft and the artisans behind it are acknowledged
and upheld.

The deck runs from the concept through design development to a Gucci-style
lookbook of embroidered kurtas, kaftans, and co-ords.
